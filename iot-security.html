Coverage

Mapped to the OWASP IoT Top 10 (2018)

Every finding is categorized against the industry-standard IoT framework, so your risk is legible to engineering, security and leadership alike.

I1

Weak, Guessable or Hardcoded Passwords

Default or embedded credentials in firmware.

I2

Insecure Network Services

Exposed, vulnerable or unnecessary services.

I3

Insecure Ecosystem Interfaces

Weak web, API, cloud or mobile interfaces.

I4

Lack of Secure Update Mechanism

Unsigned or unverified firmware updates.

I5

Use of Insecure or Outdated Components

Vulnerable libraries and OS components.

I6

Insufficient Privacy Protection

Insecure handling of user and device data.

I7

Insecure Data Transfer and Storage

Unencrypted data in transit and at rest.

I8

Lack of Device Management

No secure provisioning, monitoring or decommissioning.

I9

Insecure Default Settings

Insecure factory defaults that cannot be hardened.

I10

Lack of Physical Hardening

Exposed debug ports and accessible interfaces.
