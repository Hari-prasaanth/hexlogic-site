FAQ

Questions, answered up front

The things enterprise teams ask us before scoping. Don't see yours? Reach out — we'll answer plainly.

How long does an engagement take?
Most focused assessments run one to three weeks depending on scope and surface. We agree a timeline during scoping and keep you updated throughout — critical findings are reported the moment they're confirmed, not held until the end.
How do you price?
Engagements are fixed-price, scoped to your assets and objectives — no hourly surprises. We'll provide a clear quote after a short scoping conversation under NDA.
Will testing disrupt our production systems?
No. We agree rules of engagement before starting, work within defined windows, and favor non-destructive techniques. Where staging environments exist, we use them for higher-risk testing.
Do you re-test our fixes?
Yes — verification re-testing is included. Once your team remediates, we confirm each issue is actually closed and update the report so you have evidence for auditors and customers.
What do we actually receive?
A prioritized report with reproduction steps, evidence, business impact and remediation guidance your developers can act on, plus an executive summary. Sample reports are available for every domain we cover.
Is everything confidential?
Always. All engagements run under NDA, communications can be PGP-encrypted, and findings are handled per our responsible-disclosure and data-handling policies in the Trust Center.
