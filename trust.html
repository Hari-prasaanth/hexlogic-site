How we operate

Security commitments

The safeguards built into every HexLogic engagement.

NDA by default

Every engagement runs under a mutual NDA. Scope, findings and your identity stay confidential — before, during and after the work.

Encrypted communications

Sensitive material is shared over encrypted channels. PGP-encrypted email is available on request for findings and reports.

Data minimization

We collect only what an engagement requires, store it encrypted, and securely destroy engagement data on an agreed retention schedule.

Safe testing

Rules of engagement are agreed in writing. We favour non-destructive techniques and defined testing windows to protect production.

Vetted engineers

Work is performed only by senior, certified, background-checked engineers — never subcontracted to anonymous third parties.

Auditable evidence

Every finding ships with reproduction steps and evidence, giving you and your auditors a defensible record.

Responsible disclosure

Reporting a vulnerability

Found an issue in HexLogic's own systems? We welcome coordinated disclosure and will work with you in good faith.

Email security@hexlogic.io with details and reproduction steps. Please give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure, and avoid privacy violations, data destruction or service degradation while testing. We do not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who follow this policy.

Our machine-readable policy lives at /.well-known/security.txt.
